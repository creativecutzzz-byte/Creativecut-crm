# 15. Lead magnet: The Feature Launch Video Kit

**Status:** draft concept, not built yet. Validate it first (see "Before you build"); it takes one afternoon.

## The idea in one paragraph
A free 12-page PDF for Indian SaaS and AI startups called **"The Feature Launch Video Kit: turn every release into a LinkedIn launch in 5 days."** It gives a PMM or founder everything they need to announce their next feature with video instead of a screenshot: a 4-beat script template, five fill-in-the-blanks scripts, a 5-day launch calendar, a shot list for screen recordings, a LinkedIn post template and an honest INR cost table for doing it yourself, hiring a freelancer, a studio or a subscription. The last page offers a free 15-second teaser of their own feature, which is the same hook the outreach sequences already use. Every download becomes an inbound lead in the CRM.

## Why this one
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Is it for our buyer?** | Yes. It speaks to the ICP's own words in [01](01-niche-and-icp.md): *"We launched the feature and only announced it with a screenshot"* and *"We know we should post video on LinkedIn, but nobody owns it."* |
| **Is the pain urgent?** | It hits on the trigger we already track: *launched a new product or major feature*. A PMM with a release next Thursday searches for this the night before. |
| **Is there demand?** | Yes, there are founders publicly asking how to do this. On r/SaaS, a team said LinkedIn video *"underperformed… most people drop off in the first few seconds"* ([thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/SaaS/comments/1nh0i8c/linkedin_video_campaigns_underperforming_for_saas/)). Another founder said cutting the feature tour down to *"one clear hook and 3 core moments"* fixed their launch video ([thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/SaaSMarketing/comments/1vkb5oi/recently_made_launch_video_for_rebuttal/)). |
| **Does it lead to the sale?** | Yes. The kit shows how much work a proper launch video is. The cost table and the teaser offer lead straight into the ₹45,000 pilot ([03](03-offer-and-pricing.md)). |
| **Does it compete with free stuff?** | Yes, generic versions exist: script templates ([motionfly.co](https://motionfly.co/blog/product-launch-video-script-templates)), checklists ([guideclarity.com](https://guideclarity.com/blog/product-launch-video-checklist), [demokaze.com](https://demokaze.com/saas-launch-checklist)) and briefs ([angles.video](https://angles.video/templates/product-launch-video-brief)). They're all US-focused, tool-led (they sell an AI video generator) and stop at the script. **Our angle:** made for Indian SaaS teams, priced in INR, LinkedIn-first and covering the whole 5-day launch, not just the video. |

## The three levers (from the "market first, then build" method)
- **Angle:** for the person who has a feature shipping and no one who owns video. Not "how to make videos"; it's "how to launch this release properly".
- **Packaging:** a short, printable PDF with fill-in templates, plus a Google Doc copy of the scripts they can duplicate. It should be useful in 20 minutes.
- **Positioning:** "What we use for our own SaaS clients." It's practical, comes from people who make these videos every week and doesn't read like a sales brochure.

## Page-by-page outline
| Page | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Cover.** Title, subtitle "Turn every release into a LinkedIn launch in 5 days", the Creative Cut logo and one still from a real portfolio project. |
| 2 | **Why a screenshot isn't a launch.** Three short points: buyers watch video (66% of Indian B2B buyers say video influences purchases, [LinkedIn via exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/digital-news/66-of-b2b-buyers-in-india-say-video-influences-their-purchasing-decisions-linkedin-139021.html)), video posts are a multiplier for a good idea, not a rescue for a weak one ([study of 1,167 B2B LinkedIn posts](https://www.invisiblekeyboardai.com/blog/b2b-video-linkedin-study-2026)), and you ship monthly, so this has to be repeatable. |
| 3 | **The 4-beat launch script.** (1) The problem in one line, in the user's words. (2) The feature working: one workflow, real UI. (3) The result on screen. (4) One next step. Rules: first 3 seconds name the pain, one feature per video, captions on (most people watch muted), 30–45 seconds for LinkedIn. |
| 4–5 | **Five fill-in-the-blank scripts.** (a) New feature announcement, (b) integration launch ("Now works with ___"), (c) AI feature ("___ now writes/finds/fixes ___ for you"), (d) founder-to-camera release note, (e) Product Hunt / big launch. Each one is 6–8 lines with [brackets] and seconds marked. |
| 6 | **Screen-recording shot list.** What to capture before editing: clean demo account with realistic data (no "test test"), 1440p at 100% zoom, cursor movements slowed, each step recorded separately, the "after" state held for 3 seconds. A missing shot is the #1 cause of delays. |
| 7 | **Format cheat sheet.** 1:1 or 4:5 for the LinkedIn feed, 9:16 for Reels/Shorts, 16:9 for website and YouTube. Burned-in captions, safe zones, thumbnail frame. |
| 8 | **The 5-day launch calendar.** Day −3 teaser clip from the founder; Day −1 "something's coming" still; Day 0 launch video + changelog + email; Day +1 founder's story post ("why we built it"); Day +3 customer reaction or use case cut-down. |
| 9 | **LinkedIn post templates.** Two launch-post templates (company page and founder), with the hook line, 3 bullets, CTA and where to put the link (first comment). |
| 10 | **What it costs in India (honest table).** DIY in Canva/CapCut: ₹0 cash but 2–4 days of a PMM's time. Freelancer: about ₹3,000–₹5,000 per edited short, with 1–3 weeks of back-and-forth. Studio: ₹50,000–₹1,00,000 for one 60-second explainer ([contentcabinet.in](https://www.contentcabinet.in/insights/explainer-video-cost-india), [visualbest.co](https://www.visualbest.co/blogs/explainer-video-cost-india-global/)). Subscription: from ₹55,000 a month for a steady stream of launch content. Use the same ranges as [03](03-offer-and-pricing.md); don't overclaim. |
| 11 | **Mini case study.** One SaaS project from the portfolio, written with the template in [12](12-case-studies.md): before (screenshot launch), what we made, result. Only use real numbers with the client's permission. |
| 12 | **The offer.** "Want to see your next feature as a video? Reply with the feature and a screen recording, and we'll make a free 15-second teaser." Plus the pilot in one line, the booking link and contact details. |

**Writing rules:** short sentences, Indian English, INR everywhere, no stock "10x your growth" claims. Every statistic gets a source in the footer of that page.

## Before you build: the free validation pass (one afternoon)
Don't write 12 pages until people show they want it.
1. **Post the outline as a LinkedIn post** from the founder's account: *"We're putting together a free kit for Indian SaaS teams: 5 launch-video scripts, a 5-day LinkedIn launch calendar and real INR costs. Comment 'kit' and I'll send it when it's ready."*
2. **Send it to 10 warm contacts** in the ICP (past clients, founders you know) and ask: *"Would you use this for your next release? What's missing?"*
3. **Go/no-go:** build it if you get **15+ comments or DMs**, or **5 of the 10** say they would use it. If not, change the angle (try "AI feature launch" or "Product Hunt launch") and test again.
4. Everyone who commented is your first distribution list. Send it to them personally when it's done.

## How it reaches people
| Channel | How |
|---|---|
| **LinkedIn (main)** | The "comment 'kit'" post above, then one post a week built from a single page of the kit (a script, the calendar, the cost table), with the link in the first comment. Fits the content calendar in [09](09-marketing-content-and-ads.md). |
| **Landing page** | A section on `/landing` above the footer: "Shipping a feature soon? Get the free Launch Video Kit," with a name + work email form. |
| **Outreach** | Only as an extra in replies or follow-ups ("Happy to send our launch video kit too"). Never attach it to a cold first email; attachments hurt deliverability. |
| **Funding-news trigger** | When a prospect raises, the kit is a useful, non-salesy first touch: "Congrats on the round. When the first post-funding feature ships, this might help." |
| **Retargeting (later)** | Once the landing page has traffic, a LinkedIn ad offering the kit to site visitors. Not before the organic version converts. |

## Wiring it into the CRM
No code changes are needed to start.
- **Form:** post the download form to the existing `/api/lead-capture` endpoint with `message` set to `Lead magnet: Feature Launch Video Kit`. The CRM creates the lead (source *Website*), logs the enquiry on its timeline and adds a "Reply within 1 hour" task.
- **Delivery:** for now, the 1-hour reply task *is* the delivery. Reply personally with the PDF link and one question: *"What's the next feature you're shipping?"* That answer qualifies the lead.
- **Sequence:** start the **Inbound enquiry** sequence ([05](05-outreach-sequences.md)) only if they reply or match the ICP. Downloads that don't fit (students, agencies, consumer apps) get the PDF and nothing else.
- **Lead score:** +1 for downloading the kit, +2 more if they name a feature shipping in the next 30 days. That fits the manual check in [01](01-niche-and-icp.md).
- **Later (optional):** a separate `lead_magnet` source and an automatic email with the PDF link. Only build this once there are more than ~10 downloads a week, because the personal reply converts better.

## What to measure
