# 14. Automating repetitive work

The rule: **anything you do more than twice a week gets a template, a checklist or an automation.**

## Already automated in the CRM
| You used to… | Now the CRM… |
|---|---|
| Remember who to follow up with | Creates a dated task for every sequence step, with the message filled in (section 05) |
| Remember to chase proposals | Starts the proposal follow-up sequence when a lead moves to **Proposal** |
| Copy website enquiries into a sheet | Creates a lead from the landing page form, with a "reply within 1 hour" task |
| Plan onboarding for each client | Creates the 14-day onboarding checklist when you click **Convert to client** |
| Remember monthly reports | Creates a "Monthly report" task for every active client each month |
| Remember stalled leads | Flags overdue follow-ups on the Leads page (Overdue filter) and the dashboard |
| Type prospects in one by one | Imports a CSV of prospects (Leads → Import CSV) |

## SOP 1: New request intake (2 minutes per request)
Every client request needs the same 6 things. Use this form (Google Form, Tally or a pinned message):
1. What is it? (short-form / motion / ad variant)
2. Goal and where it'll be posted
3. Key message, in one sentence
4. Assets (link to the folder)
5. Deadline (and why)
6. Approver

Create a task in the CRM on the client's page with the request title and due date.

## SOP 2: Folder structure (same for every client)
```
Creative Cut/Clients/<Client name>/
  00_Brand/            logos, fonts, colours, brand profile
  01_Raw/              footage, screen recordings, screenshots (by date)
  02_Projects/
    2026-11_<asset-name>/
      script/  styleframes/  working/  exports/
  03_Delivered/        final files, by month
  04_Admin/            agreement, invoices, reports
```
**File naming:** `client_asset-name_format_v01.mp4`, e.g. `rocketpay_upi-autopay-explainer_16x9_v02.mp4`.

## SOP 3: Production checklist (every asset)
- [ ] Brief complete (SOP 1)
- [ ] Script approved in writing (motion pieces)
- [ ] Style frames approved (first motion piece per client, and any new style)
- [ ] Captions checked against the script, with brand and product names spelled correctly
- [ ] Music licence covers commercial use
- [ ] Exported in all agreed formats (9:16, 1:1, 16:9)
- [ ] Internal quality check by a second person
- [ ] Delivered to `03_Delivered/` with a message listing the files
- [ ] Task ticked in the CRM

## SOP 4: Invoicing and payments
- Raise all retainer invoices on the 1st, on the same day each month.
- Use your accounting tool's recurring invoices and automatic reminders (on day 3 and day 7 overdue).
- Invoices show GSTIN, SAC, place of supply and GST. Confirm the details with your accountant.

## SOP 5: Weekly admin block (Friday, 60 minutes)
1. Friday scorecard (section 13), 15 min
2. Clear overdue tasks in the CRM, 15 min
3. Update account health for every client, 10 min
4. Plan next week's content posts (section 09), 20 min

## Templates to keep ready (copy into your email tool's snippets)
- All sequence messages (section 05)
- Welcome email and kick-off agenda (section 11)
- Monthly report (section 11)
- Proposal and covering email (section 07)
- Referral ask (section 11)
- Case study permission request (section 12)
- Late payment reminder:
> "Hi [name], a gentle reminder that invoice [#] for ₹[amount] was due on [date]. Could you let me know when we can expect it? Thanks!"

## Tools that save the most time (pick what you already use)
| Job | Option |
|---|---|
| Booking calls | Google Calendar appointment schedules, or Calendly |
| Review and feedback on video | Frame.io, or timestamped comments on Google Drive |
| Recurring invoices and GST | Your accounting software (e.g. Zoho Books, QuickBooks, Tally) |
| Transcribing founder footage for captions | Your editing software's built-in transcription |
| Scheduling posts | LinkedIn's native scheduler |

Add each tool only when its job is actually slowing you down.
