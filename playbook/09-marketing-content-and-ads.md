# 9. Marketing content and ad creatives

## Content: show the work you sell (organic, 30 minutes a day)
Your product is clips, so your marketing is clips. Post on **LinkedIn** (where your ICP is) and **YouTube Shorts**.

### Weekly content plan
| Day | Format | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | **Before/after teardown**: raw section next to the finished clip, with voice-over | "Here's 30 seconds from a podcast with 4k views. Here's how we'd cut it." |
| Tue | **Hook lesson**, as a text post with 3 examples | "3 hooks that keep B2B viewers past second 2" |
| Wed | **Client clip** (with permission) plus the result | "This clip brought in 2 sales calls for [client]" |
| Thu | **Free clip for a non-client show**, tagging the host | It doubles as outreach: your sample clip, posted in public |
| Fri | **Behind the scenes**: your workflow or tools | "Our 48-hour editing pipeline, step by step" |

### 30-day launch content batch (record it all in one afternoon)
1. "Why your podcast isn't bringing in clients (and it's not the content)"
2. "The 3-second rule for B2B clips"
3. "AI clipping tools vs a human editor: same episode, side by side"
4. "How to get 30 posts from 1 podcast episode"
5. "The clip format that works on LinkedIn right now"
6. "What I'd do if I started a B2B podcast today"
7. 4 before/after teardowns of public episodes (tag the hosts; it's free outreach)

## Paid ads: start only once you have 2+ case studies and $10k MRR
Until then, every dollar is better spent on samples for outbound.

### Channel
- **LinkedIn Ads** (best targeting for the ICP; expensive): Founders, Owners or Partners at companies with 2–50 staff, in Management Consulting, Marketing Services, Professional Services or Software, plus a matched audience of your website visitors.
- **Meta retargeting**: only for people who visited your landing page or watched 50% or more of a video.
- **YouTube in-stream**: placements on popular B2B podcast channels.
- Starting budget: **$1,500/month for 30 days.** Kill any ad set whose cost per booked call exceeds **$300** (at a $1,997 plan and a 30% close rate, that's about $1,000 to acquire a customer, which pays back in about 1 month).

### Ad creative briefs (make these in Canva or CapCut)
| # | Concept | Format | Hook (first 2 seconds) | Body | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Split-screen before/after** | 9:16 video, 20s | Raw clip on the left, finished clip on the right; text: "Same 30 seconds." | "One of these gets watched. Creativecut turns your podcast into clips that bring in clients, within 48 hours." | Get 2 free clips |
| 2 | **"Your podcast is a goldmine"** | 1:1 static carousel, 5 slides | "You recorded 50 episodes. You posted 0 clips." | Slides: the problem → hours lost → 1 episode = 8 clips → client result → offer | Book a 15-minute call |
| 3 | **Founder to camera** | 9:16 video, 30s | "If you host a B2B podcast, stop scrolling." | Personal story + process + guarantee | Free sample clip |
| 4 | **Case study** (once you have one) | 4:5 static | "[Client] got 3 sales calls from one clip." | Quote + metric + face | See how |
| 5 | **Retargeting: objection killer** | 1:1 static | "'I can just use AI clipping tools.'" | Side-by-side of an AI clip and a Creativecut clip | Compare for free |

**Rules:** captions on everything, logo in the first 3 seconds for video, one message per ad, real metrics only, and test 3 hooks per concept.
