# 9. Marketing content and ad creatives

## Strategy: show, don't tell
Your buyers are SaaS founders and marketers on **LinkedIn**. The best marketing for a video studio is the videos. Post your own work and your thinking, consistently, from your **personal** profile (it reaches far more people than a company page) and repost to the Creative Cut page.

**Rhythm:** 4 posts a week from your profile, 1 per week on Instagram (reels of the work), and 1 longer breakdown a month.

## 5 content pillars
| Pillar | Example posts | Share |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Makeovers** | "We redid [public SaaS]'s feature launch as a 30-second motion video. Before and after." (Use public screenshots only, label it clearly as a concept, and don't imply they're a client.) | 30% |
| **2. Breakdowns** | "How we storyboard a 60-second SaaS explainer: 6 frames." / "3 mistakes in AI product videos" | 25% |
| **3. Launch teardowns** | "This week's best SaaS launch videos and why they work." Credit the makers. | 15% |
| **4. Proof** | Client results (with permission), behind the scenes, testimonials | 15% |
| **5. Founder and opinion** | "Why we stopped selling per-minute pricing", "What AI video tools can't do yet" | 15% |

## First 30 days of content (calendar)
| Week | Mon | Tue | Thu | Fri |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intro: "Why Creative Cut now works only with SaaS teams" | Makeover #1 (concept, labelled) | Breakdown: our 5-step process | Teardown: 3 great launch videos |
| 2 | Opinion: "Screenshots are where launches go to die" | Makeover #2 | Breakdown: scripting a 45s explainer | Behind the scenes: style frames |
| 3 | Proof: case study #1 (section 12) | Makeover #3 | Breakdown: 1 explainer → 6 social cuts | Teardown |
| 4 | Opinion: "AI video tools vs a studio: honest take" | Makeover #4 | Proof: case study #2 | Monthly recap + invite: "5 free teasers this month" |

Every post ends with one soft call to action: "DM me 'teaser' and I'll animate one of your product screens."

## Ad creatives
**Don't run cold paid ads in the first 60 days.** Outreach is cheaper and teaches you the messaging. Then start with **retargeting only** (people who visited the landing page or engaged on LinkedIn), with a small budget you can afford to test, for example ₹15,000–₹25,000 a month. Scale only what produces booked calls.

Rendered versions of the 3 creatives below are in [`landing/ads/`](../landing/ads/) (1080×1080 feed and 1080×1920 story). Replace the placeholder visual with a real 3–5 second clip from your work for the video versions.

### Creative A: "Screenshot vs launch video" (problem-aware)
- **Visual:** split screen. Left: a static feature screenshot labelled "Your launch". Right: the same UI animated, labelled "Your launch, as a video".
- **Headline:** Your feature deserves more than a screenshot.
- **Body:** Product explainers, UI animation and launch cut-downs every month, from one team that knows your product. From ₹55,000/month + GST.
- **Button:** Get a free teaser

### Creative B: "Price anchor" (solution-aware, retargeting)
- **Visual:** a crossed-out invoice line "1 explainer, ₹50k–₹1L, 3–6 weeks" next to a checklist "Explainer + 12 shorts + 4 ad variants, every month".
- **Headline:** One studio explainer, or a whole month of launch content?
- **Body:** Creative Cut's Growth plan: ₹95,000/month + GST. 3-month minimum, then month-to-month.
- **Button:** See plans

### Creative C: "Free teaser" (offer, retargeting)
- **Visual:** a phone showing a 15-second UI animation with a "15s" badge.
- **Headline:** We'll animate one of your product screens. Free.
- **Body:** Send us your product link. You'll get a 15-second motion teaser, yours to keep, whether or not we work together.
- **Button:** Send my product

### Video ad script (15 seconds, 9:16)
| Time | Visual | On-screen text / VO |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | Static screenshot, cursor hovers | "You shipped a feature." |
| 2–5s | Screenshot "unfreezes", UI animates | "Now make people care." |
| 5–10s | Fast cuts: explainer frame, founder clip with captions, ad variant | "Explainers. UI motion. Founder clips. Every month." |
| 10–13s | Price card | "From ₹55,000/month · one team that knows your product" |
| 13–15s | Logo + CTA | "Get a free 15-second teaser · creativecut.in" |

## Rules
- Never claim results you haven't measured. No "10× leads" style claims.
- Never use client logos without written permission. Use "SaaS & AI teams" instead.
- Ads go only to the landing page (section 08), never to the homepage.
