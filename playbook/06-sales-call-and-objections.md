# 6. Sales call script and objection handling

The script and objection answers are also in the CRM under **Sales kit**, so you can keep them open during calls.

## Before the call (10 minutes; the CRM creates this task when a lead moves to "Call booked")
- Watch 2 of their episodes and note their best moment and one weakness, e.g. no captions or a slow intro.
- Check their Shorts, LinkedIn and Instagram: how often they post short-form and what kind of views they get.
- Have 2–3 example clips from **their niche** ready to screen-share.
- Decide which plan you'll recommend. Present **one** plan, not three.

## The 15–20 minute call
1. **Frame (1 minute):** *"I'd like to ask a few questions about the podcast, then if it makes sense I'll show you how we'd handle it and what it costs. If it's not a fit, I'll tell you. Sound good?"*
2. **Goal (3 minutes):** *"What do you want the podcast to do for the business in the next 6 months?"* → *"Why does that matter now?"* → *"How would you measure it?"*
3. **Current state (3 minutes):** *"Walk me through what happens after you stop recording."* Who edits? How long does it take? What gets posted?
4. **Pain and cost (3 minutes):** *"What has that cost you so far?"* *"If nothing changes, where is the show in 6 months?"*
5. **Present (3 minutes):** Repeat their goal in their own words. Show what they send, what they get back, and by when. Show the examples.
6. **Price and close (2 minutes):** *"It's $1,997 a month on a 3-month initial term, then month to month. Based on what you told me, does this solve the problem?"* **Then stop talking and wait.**
   - Yes → *"Great. I'll send the agreement and invoice today, and we can kick off on [day]."* Move the lead to **Won**; onboarding starts automatically.
   - Hesitation → ask what's behind it (see below).
   - They need to think → agree a specific follow-up date **on the call**. Move the lead to **Proposal sent** so follow-ups are scheduled, then send the recap email (the **"After the call"** template).

## Objection handling
Use this pattern for every objection: **acknowledge → ask a question → answer → check.** Don't argue.

| Objection | What it usually means | Response |
|---|---|---|
| "It's too expensive." | They don't see the value yet, or they're comparing you with a freelancer | "Compared to what?" Then: "If this took 8 hours a week off your plate, what's an hour of your time worth?" At $150/hour, 32 hours a month is $4,800. Offer the **Clips plan** rather than discounting Engine. |
| "I already have an editor." | Long-form is handled; clips aren't | "What do they handle, and what still lands on you?" Offer clips only, working alongside their editor. |
| "I'll use AI clipping tools." | Price anchoring | "We use them too, for speed. The difference is judgement: choosing the moments, writing hooks, keeping it on-brand. Want to compare one of your AI clips with one of ours?" |
| "Short-form doesn't work in my industry." | Fear of looking unprofessional | "Which platforms have you tested, and for how long?" Show B2B accounts in their niche that are growing. Offer the **$500 Pilot**. |
| "I need to think about it." | Something is unclear, or it's the price | "Of course. Usually that means something's unclear or it's the price. Which is it for you?" |
| "Send me some info." | A polite brush-off | "Happy to. So I don't send you a generic deck, what would you need to see to say yes?" Book a 10-minute follow-up. |
| "Not right now." | A timing or priority problem | "What changes next quarter?" If something real, create a task for that date. If not, offer the Pilot. |
| "What if I don't like the edits?" | Fear of risk | Two revision rounds per video, a style guide that improves every week, and a 30-day exit if we miss turnaround. |
| "Can you do it cheaper?" | Testing you | "I can do less for less. Clips is $997 and drops the long-form edit. I don't discount Engine, because that's what keeps quality high." |

After every call, log one line in the lead's **Activity**: the objection, what you said, and the outcome. Review these monthly and update this table.
