# 1. Niche and ideal customer

## The niche
**Funded Indian B2B SaaS and AI startups (Seed to Series B) that need product and launch content every month.**

### Why this niche fits Creative Cut
| Factor | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **You already have the proof** | creativecut.in shows 17 motion and SaaS explainer projects plus 10 video-editing projects. Proof is the hardest thing for a new agency to get, and you already have it for this niche. |
| **They can pay** | A funded startup has a marketing budget. In India, a single 60-second SaaS demo video costs ₹50,000 to ₹1,00,000 from a studio ([contentcabinet.in](https://www.contentcabinet.in/insights/explainer-video-cost-india), [visualbest.co](https://www.visualbest.co/blogs/explainer-video-cost-india-global/)). A subscription that delivers more for a similar monthly figure is an easy comparison. |
| **The need repeats** | SaaS companies ship features constantly. Every launch needs a video, cut-downs for LinkedIn and Instagram, and founder content. Recurring need makes a subscription natural. |
| **Video matters to their buyers** | LinkedIn reported that 66% of B2B buyers in India say video influences their purchase decisions ([exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/digital-news/66-of-b2b-buyers-in-india-say-video-influences-their-purchasing-decisions-linkedin-139021.html)). |
| **They're easy to find** | Every funding round is published (Inc42, Entrackr, YourStory). Funding news gives you a ready-made reason to reach out. |
| **City doesn't matter** | UI animation and editing are done remotely, so you can sell across Bengaluru, Pune, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad and Chennai. |
| **The market is large** | Tracxn lists 45,653 SaaS companies in India, about 4,100 of which have raised funding (October 2026, [tracxn.com](https://tracxn.com/d/explore/saas-startups-in-india/__tiPN56JiWzxgJ8waREJOEFNqn9n0tsWv5RLE38yWlW0)). Every week, roughly 15–30 Indian startups announce funding rounds, and several of them are B2B tech. |

## Ideal customer profile
| Attribute | Ideal | Acceptable | Disqualify |
|---|---|---|---|
| Company | B2B SaaS, AI or devtools startup selling to businesses | B2B fintech infrastructure, HR tech, vertical SaaS | Consumer apps, D2C, services agencies |
| Stage | Seed to Series B, raised in the last 12 months | Bootstrapped with ₹5 crore+ revenue | Pre-revenue with no funding |
| Team | 10–150 people | 5–10 people | 500+ (procurement-led, slow) |
| Marketing | Has 1–3 marketers (PMM, content or growth), but no in-house motion designer | Founder does the marketing | Has an in-house video team |
| Current video | No product video, or one that's more than a year old. LinkedIn posts with no video. | Some video, but low quality or inconsistent | Already posts polished video weekly |
| Buyer | Founder or CEO (seed), Head of Marketing or PMM (Series A/B) | Growth lead | Intern or junior marketer |
| Market | Sells to India, the US or global (either way, they pay you in INR) | | |

### Who signs, and what each person cares about
- **Founder or CEO (Seed):** wants to look bigger than they are, needs launch videos for investors and customers, and has no time.
- **Head of Marketing or PMM (Series A/B):** needs a reliable pipeline of assets for every launch, gets judged on pipeline and launch impact, and hates managing freelancers.
- **Growth lead:** needs ad creative variants to test, and fast.

### Their words (use them in your copy)
- "We launched the feature and only announced it with a screenshot."
- "Our product video is from two versions ago."
- "Freelancers take three weeks and need explaining every time."
- "We know we should post video on LinkedIn, but nobody owns it."
- "Agencies quote ₹1 lakh for one minute. We need something every month."

### What they really buy
Not videos. They buy **(1) launches that people notice, (2) a brand that looks funded and credible, and (3) a team that already understands the product, so there's no re-briefing.** Sell those three outcomes everywhere.

## Buying triggers (reach out within days of these)
1. **Raised funding** in the last 90 days. This is the strongest trigger, because the money is earmarked for growth.
2. **Hiring** a Product Marketing Manager, Content Marketer, Brand Designer or Video Editor (LinkedIn Jobs, Wellfound).
3. **Launched** a new product or major feature (Product Hunt, their changelog, a LinkedIn post).
4. **Rebranded** or redesigned their website.
5. **Event coming up**, such as SaaSBOOMi, Nasscom events or a demo day, where they need a sizzle video.

## Lead score
The CRM scores leads automatically on source, value and engagement. Add this manual check in the lead's notes before you start a sequence. **6 or more = reach out this week.**
- +3 Raised funding in the last 90 days
- +2 No product video on the homepage, or one that's clearly outdated
- +2 Hiring a marketing or design role
- +1 Founder or team posts on LinkedIn without video
- +1 Launched something in the last 30 days
- −3 Already has an in-house motion designer, or posts polished video weekly
