# 1. Niche and ideal customer

## The niche: turning B2B podcasts into short-form clips

**Creativecut turns one podcast or long-form video episode a week into a full YouTube edit plus a week of short clips for founders, consultants and coaches who sell high-ticket B2B services.**

### Why this niche
| Factor | Why it suits a new agency |
|---|---|
| **Ability to pay** | A single client for a consultant or B2B founder is often worth $5k–$50k+. A $2k/month editing bill is small next to that. Creators who live on AdSense are far more price-sensitive. |
| **Recurring work** | Podcasts publish weekly, so the work naturally fits a monthly retainer. No project-by-project selling. |
| **Clear pain** | The owner records and then nothing happens. The editing backlog costs them 5–10 hours a week, or the episodes sit unused. |
| **Easy to show value before they pay** | You can cut a free sample clip from their public episode before you ever speak, which is the strongest cold-outreach hook there is. |
| **Easy to find** | Podcasts are public and searchable. Every lead comes with a ready-made personalization line: something specific they said on a recent episode. |
| **Room to specialise** | Most competitors are generalist "unlimited editing" subscriptions. Owning one niche lets you charge more and write sharper copy. |

### Niches considered and rejected (for now)
- **YouTube creators (entertainment, gaming, vlogs):** large market, but price-sensitive, high churn and crowded with $500/month subscriptions.
- **Local businesses (restaurants, gyms):** low budgets, rarely produce long-form content, and need filming as well as editing.
- **Wedding and event video:** project-based, seasonal and needs on-site shooting.
- **Real-estate agents:** a workable niche, but they need filming support and churn with the market. A good second niche once operations are stable.

## Ideal customer profile (ICP)

| Attribute | Ideal | Acceptable | Disqualify |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business | B2B consultant, agency owner, coach selling $3k+ offers, SaaS founder, fractional exec, professional services (legal, finance, recruiting) | B2B newsletter or community owner | B2C hobby show, no business behind the show |
| Content | Video podcast or YouTube show, publishing weekly, 10+ episodes so far | Every 2 weeks, audio-only with plans to add video | Fewer than 5 episodes, or stopped publishing 60+ days ago |
| Current short-form | None, or inconsistent (a few clips a month, obviously AI auto-clipped) | Posting, but with poor hooks or no captions | Already has a strong in-house team posting daily |
| Revenue | ~$500k–$10M/year (5–50 staff) | Solo operator charging $5k+ per client | Pre-revenue |
| Decision maker | Founder or host (they sign the cheque) | Head of marketing | Junior assistant with no budget |
| Geography | US, UK, Canada, Australia, NZ, EU (English-language shows) | Any English-language show | — |

### Their language (use it in your copy)
- "I record the episode and then it just sits there."
- "I know I should be on LinkedIn/Shorts but I don't have time."
- "My last editor ghosted me."
- "We tried an AI clipping tool. The clips were random and looked cheap."
- "I want the podcast to bring in clients, not just downloads."

### What they actually buy
They aren't buying edits. They are buying: **(1) hours back every week, (2) a consistent presence where their buyers scroll, and (3) more inbound conversations that lead to sales.** Every page, email and call should sell those three outcomes.

## Lead scoring (use the CRM's "Notes" field or a tag in the Source field)
Add points; anyone at **6 or more** goes into a sequence this week.
- +3 Publishes weekly video
- +2 Sells a high-ticket B2B offer
- +2 No short-form clips on LinkedIn or YouTube Shorts in the last 30 days
- +1 Episode views under 2k (room to grow, and they can see it)
- +1 Has posted recently about growth, hiring or marketing
