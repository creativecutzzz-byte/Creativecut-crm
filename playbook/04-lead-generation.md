# 4. Finding customers and generating leads

## Where your ICP lives
| Source | How to search | Personalization you'll get |
|---|---|---|
| **YouTube** | Search `"podcast" + [niche]`, e.g. "B2B sales podcast", "agency owner podcast", "fractional CFO podcast". Filter by upload date: this month. Shortlist channels with 500–50k subscribers and weekly uploads. | A specific moment in their latest episode |
| **Apple Podcasts / Spotify charts** | Categories Business → Entrepreneurship, Management, Marketing. Look for a "Video" badge on Spotify. | Episode title and guest |
| **LinkedIn** | Search posts for "new episode", "on the pod", "latest episode" + niche keywords. Sales Navigator: title contains "Host" OR "Founder", headline contains "podcast". | Their own post about the episode |
| **Podcast guest-booking directories** | PodMatch, Podcast Guests, MatchMaker.fm. Hosts list their shows there. | Show topic and audience |
| **Apollo / Clay / Vibe Prospecting** | Filter: industry = Management Consulting, Marketing & Advertising, Professional Services, Software; employees 2–50; keywords "podcast"; titles Founder/CEO/Owner/Managing Partner. Use these to **find the email** after you've found the show. | Company size and industry |
| **Your own network** | Everyone you know who hosts or guests on a show. Warm intros close 3–5 times better. | Personal |

## Daily prospecting routine (60–90 minutes, 20 leads a day)
1. Find 20 shows that fit the ICP (`01-niche-and-icp.md`, score 6 or more).
2. For each, watch the first 3 minutes plus one random section of the latest episode. Write **one specific personalization line**, e.g.:
   - *"Your point on episode 112 that 'discounting is a positioning problem' deserves a clip of its own; it's the best 30 seconds of the episode."*
   - Avoid generic praise ("love your content!"). If you can't write a specific line, skip the lead.
3. Find the founder or host's email (Apollo, Hunter, the website contact page, or the podcast's RSS feed, which often lists an owner email).
4. Add them to a CSV with the columns `name, company, email, channel, niche, source, personalization, website` and import it from **CRM → Leads → Import CSV**.
5. In the CRM, open each lead and **Enroll → "Cold email: podcast founders"**. Follow-ups schedule automatically.
6. Each week, pick the **5 best-fit leads** and cut 1 free sample clip each (step 3 of the sequence). Samples drive most of your replies.

## Email deliverability (set this up before you send a single cold email)
- **Never cold-email from your main domain.** Buy 2–3 lookalike domains (e.g. `trycreativecut.com`, `creativecut.co`) and forward them to your real site.
- Set up **SPF, DKIM and DMARC** on each sending domain, using Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 inboxes.
- **Warm up** each inbox for 2–3 weeks with a warm-up tool before sending cold.
- Send at most **30–40 cold emails per inbox per day**. Use plain text, at most 1 link, and no images or tracking pixels in the first email.
- Check your list with an email verifier and only send to verified addresses. Keep your bounce rate under 2%.
- **Comply with the law.** Include your real business name and a postal address, honour every opt-out immediately, and don't use misleading subject lines (US CAN-SPAM). For prospects in the UK or EU, cold B2B email is lawful under "legitimate interest" only if it is relevant to their role, gives an easy opt-out, and you keep a record of where you got their data. **If in doubt, check with a lawyer for the countries you target.**

## Other lead channels (add once cold outbound is running)
1. **Referrals:** the CRM schedules a referral ask at day 45 of every client. Offer 20% off a month for each referral that signs.
2. **Partnerships:** podcast production studios that only record, podcast guest-booking agencies, and B2B marketing agencies without in-house video. Offer a 10% recurring referral fee.
3. **Content (inbound):** see `09-marketing-content-and-ads.md`.
4. **Paid ads:** only after you have 2 or more case studies (`09-marketing-content-and-ads.md`).
