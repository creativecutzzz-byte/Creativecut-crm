# 2. Competitive landscape

> Prices below come from third-party listings and vendor pages found in October 2026. They change often, and some sources disagree with each other. **Check each competitor's own pricing page before you quote against them.**

## Who you're up against

| Competitor type | Examples | Price signal | Strength | Weakness you exploit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| "Unlimited" editing subscriptions | VidChops, Video Husky | VidChops reported at about $495/month (Weekly) and about $995/month (Pro) [1][2]. Video Husky reported from about $849/month [3], with one roundup saying $1,000–$2,500 [4] | Established brands, volume, many editors | Generalists. No niche strategy. Queue-based turnaround. The client still has to decide what to clip and write the hooks. |
| Podcast clipping micro-agencies | Clip Craft and many Whop/Skool sellers | About $397/month for clips only; about $997/month with long-form and a strategy call [5][6]. Others at $897/month with a 3-month minimum [7] | Cheap, fast | Thin on strategy, often output from AI tools, high churn, little proof |
| Freelancers | Upwork, Fiverr, referrals | $15–$60/hour, or per video | Cheapest, flexible | Unreliable ("my editor ghosted me"), no system, the owner has to project-manage them |
| AI clipping tools | Opus Clip, Descript, Riverside Magic Clips | $0–$50/month | Near-free, instant | Picks moments with no judgement, generic look, the founder still has to review and post. **You use these tools inside your process; you don't compete with them on price.** |
| Full-service podcast production houses | Studio and recording agencies | $8,000–$15,000+/month, including recording [8] | Done for you end to end | Far too expensive for most of your ICP. Sits above you, so it makes your price look reasonable. |

## Where Creativecut is positioned
**Specialist, not a commodity.** We are priced above the clipping micro-agencies and around the top of the subscription services, and justify it with:
1. **One niche only.** Hooks, titles and examples are written for B2B buyers, not for entertainment audiences.
2. **Editorial judgement included.** We pick the moments, write the hooks and titles, and send a monthly "what worked" note. The client makes zero decisions.
3. **48-hour turnaround promise**, written into the agreement with a 30-day exit if we miss it.
4. **Outcome reporting.** Views and follower growth, plus "conversations started", tracked monthly.
