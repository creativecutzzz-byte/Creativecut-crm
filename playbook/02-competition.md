# 2. Competition

When a SaaS startup needs video, it has six alternatives to Creative Cut. Prices come from published 2026 rate cards and guides (sources at the bottom). Most are agency blogs, so treat them as ranges.

| Alternative | Typical price | Strength | Weakness you exploit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SaaS explainer studios in India** (e.g. Studiotale in Bengaluru, Motiongility, MyPromoVideos, Broadcast2World in Gurugram) | ₹50,000–₹1,00,000 per 60-second demo; ₹40,000–₹90,000 per minute of 2D motion | Polished, specialised | Priced per project. Slow (3–6 weeks). Re-briefing every time. Nothing for social or founder content. |
| **Global SaaS video subscriptions and agencies** (Content Beta, Superside, Vidico, Thinkmojo) | Priced in USD. | **Indian social media agencies** | ₹25,000–₹80,000/month for 4–12 reels | Cheap, handle posting | Generalists. No UI animation or product explainers. They don't understand SaaS. |
| **Freelancers** (Upwork, Fiverr, referrals) | ₹15,000–₹60,000 per project | Cheapest | Unreliable, they disappear, and quality varies. The founder ends up managing them. |
| **An in-house editor or motion designer** | Monthly salary plus tools and hiring time. Get your own salary data before quoting it. | Always available | One person, one skill set. Usually either an editor or a motion designer, rarely both. Hard to hire for a seed-stage company. |
| **AI video tools** (AI demo-video and avatar platforms; Clueso, for example, raised $10M in October 2026) | SaaS subscription | Fast and cheap for simple walkthroughs | Template-looking output, no creative direction, still needs someone to run it. You use AI where it helps; see "AI-Enhanced Content" on your site. |

## Your position
**"The SaaS-native creative subscription, priced in rupees."**
One team that learns your product once, then ships launch videos, UI motion, founder content and ad creatives every month, for about the cost of one studio explainer.

### Why Creative Cut wins
1. **Product understanding is kept.** You learn their product once, and every brief builds on it. Your site already says this ("We learn your brand once").
2. **Both skill sets in one place.** Motion (explainers, UI animation) and editing (founder reels, LinkedIn video). Studios do one; social agencies do the other.
3. **Priced for Indian startups.** INR pricing with GST invoices, versus USD subscriptions.
4. **Speed.** Short-form in 48–72 hours, feature videos in 7–10 working days, versus 3–6 weeks at studios.
5. **Proof.** 17 motion and SaaS explainer projects, and 25+ brands and creators.

### Where you shouldn't compete
- **Live-action brand films with big crews.** Refer these out, or partner on them.
- **Cheapest price.** Never compete with ₹15,000 freelancers. Compete on reliability and product understanding.
- **"Unlimited" plans.** These attract abuse. Define clear monthly deliverables (see section 03).

## Watch list (check quarterly)
- Content Beta and other SaaS video subscriptions: their pricing pages and any India-specific offer.
- AI demo-video tools: what they can now do well enough that clients stop paying humans for it. Move up to strategy, story and motion quality.
- New Indian studios advertising "SaaS video" on LinkedIn.
