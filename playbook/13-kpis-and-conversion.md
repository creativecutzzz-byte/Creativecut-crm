# 13. KPIs, tracking and improving conversion

## The funnel and target conversion rates
These are **starting assumptions** for planning. Replace them with your own numbers after 4 weeks; the CRM dashboard shows your actual stage-to-stage conversion.

| Stage | Weekly target | Assumed conversion to next stage |
|---|---|---|
| Prospects added | 50 | 80% get a sequence started |
| Sequences started | 40 | 10% reply positively |
| Positive replies | 4 | 50% book a call |
| Discovery calls | 2 | 60% get a proposal |
| Proposals sent | ~1.2 | 40% win (pilot or plan) |
| **New clients** | **~0.5/week, about 2 a month** | 60% of pilots convert to a plan |

**So, to reach 5 clients in 90 days** you need roughly 600 prospects, 480 sequences, 48 positive replies, 24 calls and 14 proposals. If you're behind, the fix is almost always **more volume at the top, or a better step 1 email**, not a lower price.

## When a number is low, fix this
| Low number | Likely cause | Fix (one change at a time, test for 2 weeks) |
|---|---|---|
| Reply rate < 5% | Generic first line, wrong person, or spam folder | Rewrite the personalisation; check you're reaching the founder or PMM; test inbox placement; send the 15-second teaser as touch 1 to the top prospects |
| Replies but few calls | The ask is too big | Offer the teaser first, then the call. Send the booking link only after interest. |
| Calls but few proposals | Wrong-fit leads, or not enough pain uncovered | Tighten the ideal customer profile (section 01); ask more problem and impact questions on the call (section 06) |
| Proposals but few wins | Price shock, or no urgency | Always offer the pilot; tie the proposal to their launch date; follow up with the sequence |
| Pilots don't convert to plans | Pilot experience or no plan for next month | On pilot delivery day, present the next 3 months of content already mapped to their roadmap |
| Clients churn after 3 months | Weak onboarding or unused deliverables | Section 11: first delivery within 7 days, monthly report, a proactive plan |

## Conversion rate optimisation checklist
**Outreach**
- [ ] First line is about them, not you
- [ ] One clear, low-effort ask per message
- [ ] Teaser offered by touch 2

**Landing page**
- [ ] One goal (teaser form), repeated at the top, middle and end
- [ ] Prices visible (this filters out bad fits and builds trust)
- [ ] Real work samples above the fold as soon as they're approved
- [ ] Form has 5 fields or fewer

**Calls and proposals**
- [ ] Proposal sent within 2 hours
- [ ] Next meeting booked before the call ends
- [ ] The pilot is always the alternative option

**Retention**
- [ ] First delivery within 7 days of kick-off
- [ ] Monthly report sent by the 28th
- [ ] Quarterly review with an upgrade or renewal proposal
