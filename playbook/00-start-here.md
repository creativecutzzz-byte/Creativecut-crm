# Creative Cut go-to-market playbook

**Business:** Creative Cut, a creative subscription for video and motion content.
**Market:** India, priced in INR (+18% GST).
**Niche:** funded Indian B2B SaaS and AI startups, Seed to Series B.
**Prepared:** October 2026.

This folder is the business plan. The CRM in this repository runs it: sequences, automations, tasks and the dashboard all follow these documents.

## The plan in one paragraph
Indian SaaS and AI startups ship a feature most months, and each launch needs a product video, social cut-downs and founder content. Today they pay ₹50,000 to ₹1,00,000 for a single 60-second explainer from a studio, or they rely on unreliable freelancers. Creative Cut sells them a monthly creative subscription: explainers, UI motion, founder reels and ad creatives from one team that already knows their product. It costs less than buying the same output piece by piece. You win the first deal with proof (17 SaaS and motion projects already in the portfolio) and a free 15-second teaser of their own product. You grow by keeping clients for 12+ months and asking for referrals.

## First 30 days
| Week | Do this | Docs |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Set up** | Connect Supabase so the CRM is live. Publish the `/landing` page. Pick 3 SaaS projects and write them up as case studies. Set up a Google Workspace inbox on a secondary domain and start warming it up. Optimise your LinkedIn profile. | 03, 08, 12, 14 |
| **2. Prospect** | Import the starter list. Add 10 new prospects a day from funding news and job posts. Make a 15-second teaser for the 5 best prospects. Start sequences. Post on LinkedIn daily. | 04, 05, 09 |
| **3. Sell** | Run discovery calls with the script. Send proposals within 2 hours. Offer founding-client pricing to the first 5 clients. Clear the CRM's Tasks page every morning. | 06, 07, 10 |
| **4. Deliver and measure** | Onboard each new client with the CRM's automatic checklist. Deliver the first asset within 7 days. Fill in the Friday scorecard and fix the weakest funnel stage. | 11, 13 |

**First 90-day target:** 5 paying clients (3 Starter + 2 Growth), which is ₹3,55,000 MRR. Sections 03 and 13 show the maths.

## What I couldn't do for you
- **Contact details.** The prospect list has verified companies, what they do and their funding. Founder names and emails should come from LinkedIn or the company site. A lead-data connector such as Apollo needs your authorisation first.
- **Legal review.** Cold outreach in India falls under the DPDP Act 2023. How it applies to B2B prospecting isn't settled yet, so section 05 sets conservative rules. Have a lawyer check your contract and privacy policy before you sign clients.
- **Numbers you haven't measured yet.** Conversion rates in section 13 are planning assumptions. Replace them with your own figures after 4 weeks.
