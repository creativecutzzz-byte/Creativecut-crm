# 4. Finding customers and generating leads

## Starter list: 11 verified prospects
[`prospects-2026-10.csv`](prospects-2026-10.csv) lists Indian B2B software and AI startups that announced funding between 28 September and 16 October 2026. Each was checked against the funding report and its own website. Each row has what the company does, the round, the lead investor, the source link and a ready personalisation line.

- Two websites couldn't be verified (**Shamrock AI**: `shamrock.ai` is a parked domain; **Unveilr AI**: `unveilr.ai` belongs to a different company). Find their real sites from the founders' LinkedIn profiles before contacting them.
- **Excluded on purpose:** Clueso (an AI video-creation platform, so a substitute for you, not a buyer); Confido Health (US-headquartered, sells to US clinics); a "Scooter" AI deal (no verifiable website).
- The list has no personal emails or phone numbers. Find the right person on LinkedIn (founder at seed stage, Head of Marketing or PMM at Series A/B). Use their work email only if it's published or you've confirmed it.

**Import it:** in the CRM, go to **Leads → Import CSV** and choose `prospects-2026-10.csv`. Each company becomes a lead (source "Other") with its personalisation line filled in. The research (what they do, funding, investor, source link) is saved as a note on its timeline. Then add the contact person's name with **Edit**. Until you do, messages open with "Hi there".

## Daily prospecting routine (45 minutes, every working day)
1. **10 min:** check yesterday's funding news and new job posts. Add 5–10 companies that match the ideal customer profile (section 01).
2. **15 min:** for each one, find the decision-maker on LinkedIn, check the homepage for a product video, and write one personalisation line (formula in section 05).
3. **10 min:** score them (section 01). Anyone scoring 6+ gets enrolled in an outreach sequence in the CRM.
4. **10 min:** for the best 1–2 prospects of the week, brief a **15-second teaser**: animate one screen of their product from public screenshots. Send it as the first touch. It takes about 1–2 hours of motion time and is your strongest hook.

**Weekly target:** 50 new prospects added, 40 sequences started, 2 teasers sent.

## Prospect research checklist (for the lead's notes)
- [ ] What they sell, in one line, in their words
- [ ] Funding: round, date, lead investor
- [ ] Decision-maker name and title (LinkedIn)
- [ ] Is there a product video on the homepage? How old is it?
- [ ] Their last 5 LinkedIn posts: any video?
- [ ] Recent launch or feature (changelog, Product Hunt, LinkedIn)
- [ ] Open marketing or design roles
- [ ] Personalisation line written

## Data and privacy rules
India's DPDP Act 2023 covers business contact data, and whether it allows cold B2B outreach without consent isn't settled yet ([complydp.com](https://www.complydp.com/articles/does-dpdp-apply-to-b2b-customer-data), [smtpgrid.io](https://smtpgrid.io/blog/dpdp-rules-email-senders-india)). To stay on the safe side:
- Only collect what you need: name, role, company and work email. No personal phone numbers.
- Use only sources the person made public themselves (LinkedIn, the company site, published press releases).
- Say who you are and why you're writing. Give a clear way to opt out, and honour it immediately. The CRM's "Lost" stage plus a note "opted out" means you never contact them again.
- Delete prospects who don't respond after the full sequence plus 90 days.
- Have a lawyer review this approach before you scale past about 50 emails a day.
