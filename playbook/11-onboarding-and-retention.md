# 11. Client onboarding and retention

Retention is where the money is. A Growth client who stays 12 months is worth ₹11.4 lakh. One who leaves after 3 months is worth ₹2.85 lakh. Onboarding is where most churn is decided.

## Onboarding: first 14 days
When you click **Convert to client** on a lead, the CRM creates these tasks automatically on the new client, with due dates.

| Day | Task | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Send welcome email + agreement + first invoice | You |
| 0 | Create the client's shared folder (structure in section 14) and request assets | You |
| 1 | Kick-off call (45 min): brand profile, product walkthrough, launch calendar | You + creative lead |
| 2 | Send the brand and product profile document for approval | Creative lead |
| 3 | Agree the first month's content plan (what, when, in what order) | You |
| 5 | Script for motion piece #1 approved | Client |
| 7 | **First delivery**: a short-form batch or style frames. Early proof matters more than anything else this month. | Team |
| 10 | Check-in message: "How's it going so far? Anything we should change?" | You |
| 14 | Motion piece #1 delivered; collect feedback | Team |

### Welcome email
> Subject: Welcome to Creative Cut, {{company}} 🎬
>
> Hi {{first_name}}, we're really glad to be working with you.
>
> What happens next:
> 1. **Kick-off call**: please pick a slot here: {{booking_link}}. Bring your product login (or screenshots), brand assets and your launch calendar.
> 2. **Shared folder**: I'll send a link today. Drop footage and assets there anytime.
> 3. **First delivery**: you'll have your first content within 7 days of kick-off.
>
> Your agreement and first invoice are attached. Any questions, just reply. I'm your single point of contact.
>
> {{sender_name}}

### Kick-off agenda (45 min)
1. Goals for 90 days, in their words (10 min)
2. Product walkthrough: the 3 features that matter most to buyers (10 min)
3. Brand: tone, 3 references they love, 3 they hate, do's and don'ts (10 min)
4. Launch calendar for the next 8 weeks (5 min)
5. How we work: requests, review rounds, turnaround, one approver (5 min)
6. Agree the first two deliverables and dates (5 min)

## Monthly rhythm (every client)
| When | What |
|---|---|
| Week 1 | Monthly planning call (Growth and Scale) or async plan (Starter) |
| Ongoing | Deliveries according to the plan's turnaround |
| Last week | **Monthly report** (template below) |
| Every 3 months | Quarterly review: results, what to change, propose an upgrade if they're consistently at their plan's limit |

The CRM creates a "Monthly report" task for every active client on the 25th of each month (section 10).

### Monthly report template (one page)
- **Delivered this month:** X short-form videos, Y motion pieces, Z ad variants (vs plan)
- **Highlights:** the top 2 performing pieces and their numbers. Ask the client for views, engagement and leads, or use their analytics access.
- **What we learned:** 1–2 lines
- **Next month's plan:** dates and priorities
- **One suggestion:** something proactive, such as a format to try or a launch to prepare

## Churn warning signs → what to do
| Signal | Action, within 48 hours |
|---|---|
| Approvals take more than 5 days, twice | Call: "Is the approval process working? Should we change who signs off?" |
| They use less than 60% of the monthly deliverables | Offer to plan the month for them, or suggest moving down a plan before they cancel |
| Their champion leaves the company | Meet the new owner within a week. Re-do the kick-off. |
| Two revision rounds on the same asset, twice | Review the brief process. Add style frames before animation. |
| Late payment | Polite reminder on day 3, call on day 7, pause work after 7 days overdue (per contract) |
| Account health in the CRM below 60 | Owner's call this week |

Update **Account health** on the client in the CRM monthly. 80+ is happy, 60–79 is watch, below 60 is at risk.

## Renewals and upgrades
- **Month 2, week 3** (before the 3-month minimum ends): send a short "quarter so far" summary and propose the next 3 months.
- Upgrade trigger: they've hit their plan's limits two months in a row or bought add-ons twice → propose the next plan with the exact saving.

## Referrals
Ask at a high point, such as after a launch goes well or after a positive message from them. Never ask in month 1.
> "Really glad the [launch] landed well! Quick question: do you know one or two other founders or marketers who are drowning in launches? If you intro us, we'll give them a free teaser, and you get one extra motion piece on us next month."

Track referrals by setting the lead's source to **Referral** in the CRM.

## Testimonials and case studies
After the first motion piece is approved and published, ask for a 2-line quote. After 3 months, ask for a case study (section 12).
