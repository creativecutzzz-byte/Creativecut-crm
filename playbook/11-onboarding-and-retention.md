# 11. Client onboarding and retention

## Onboarding: first delivery within 7 days
The checklist below is built into **CRM → Clients**, and the tasks are created automatically when a lead moves to **Won**.

1. Agreement signed (scope, revisions, payment terms)
2. First payment received or subscription active
3. Onboarding form completed (see the questions below)
4. Brand kit received: logo, fonts, colours, intro/outro, music preferences
5. Shared upload folder set up, with a naming convention (`YYYY-MM-DD_EpisodeNumber_Guest`)
6. Platform access, if we post for them
7. Kickoff call held; style guide written and shared with the editor
8. First batch delivered within 7 days
9. Feedback on the first batch added to the style guide
10. Weekly delivery day and monthly report date agreed

### Onboarding form questions
1. What should the podcast do for your business in 6 months?
2. Who is your ideal listener (job title, industry, problem)?
3. Which platforms should the clips go on? Who posts them?
4. Send links to 3 clips or creators whose style you love, and 1 you dislike.
5. Words, topics or competitors we should never mention?
6. Brand assets: logo, fonts, colours, intro/outro, music.
7. Who approves edits, and how quickly can they turn feedback around?
8. Your recording setup (Riverside, Zoom or camera) and when episodes usually arrive.

### Kickoff call agenda (30 minutes)
Goals (5 minutes) → audience and positioning (5) → style: go through the examples they love and dislike (10) → workflow: upload, delivery, approval, posting (5) → first-batch plan and dates (5).

## Retention: keep clients for 12+ months
Most agency churn happens in **months 2–4**, when the novelty has worn off and results aren't visible yet. Plan for it.

| When | What | Template |
|---|---|---|
| Every delivery | 2-line note: "Top pick this week is clip #3, because…" | — |
| Day 14 | Check-in, with a 1–10 score: "What would make it a 10?" | `checkin_2w` |
| Monthly | "What worked" report: top 3 clips, views and follower growth, conversations started, and next month's test | — |
| Day 45 | Testimonial and referral ask | `referral_ask` |
| Day 60 | Case-study permission | `case_study_ask` |
| 14 days before renewal | Renewal call with results recap and a recommended plan | `renewal` |
| After churn + 30 days | Win-back offer | `winback` |

### Health score (set it in CRM → Clients)
- **Good:** approves quickly, posts consistently, positive feedback.
- **At risk:** slow approvals, clips not being posted, payment late, or a contact goes quiet for 2+ weeks. **Act within 48 hours:** book a call and change something visible (hook style, format, platform).
- **Unhappy:** complaints or a mention of cancelling. Call the same day. Offer a free week or a plan change before you offer a discount.

### Retention levers
- **Make results visible.** Clients cancel when they can't see results, not when results are poor. Send the monthly report even when the numbers are small.
- **Same editor every week.** Consistency of style is the main thing clients are paying for.
- **Quarterly "next level" idea**, e.g. a new format, a guest-clip pack or a newsletter, to keep the relationship moving forward.
- **Annual option:** 2 months free when paid upfront, offered at the first renewal only.
