# 8. Landing page (copy and structure)

One page with one goal: **book a 15-minute call** (or request free sample clips). Build it in Framer, Webflow, Carrd or Wix in an afternoon, using the copy below.

---

**[Hero]**
# Your podcast, turned into a week of clips that bring in clients.
Record your episode. Within 48 hours you get a polished YouTube edit and a week of captioned short clips for LinkedIn, Shorts and Reels. You never touch editing again.
**[Get 2 free clips from your latest episode →]** · *Built for B2B founders, consultants and coaches.*

**[Proof bar]**
Logos or names of your first clients · "48-hour turnaround" · "X clips delivered"
*(Leave this out until you have real numbers. Never invent proof.)*

**[Problem]**
## You record a great episode… and then it sits there.
- Editing eats 5–10 hours of your week, or it doesn't get done at all.
- Your best ideas are buried at minute 37, where nobody hears them.
- AI clipping tools pick random moments and make you look generic.

**[How it works]**
1. **Upload.** Drop your raw recording in your shared folder.
2. **We edit.** Full YouTube edit plus hook-first, captioned vertical clips, with titles and captions written for you.
3. **You post.** Or, on Studio, we post for you. Every month we send a "what worked" report.

**[Before/after]** Show one raw 30-second section next to the finished clip.

**[Pricing]** The three plans from `03-offer-and-pricing.md`, with **Engine** highlighted as "Most popular". Show prices; being open about price filters out poor-fit leads.

**[Guarantee]**
48-hour turnaround, or cancel any time that month. 2 revision rounds per video. You own everything.

**[Case studies]** 2–3 short cards: client, problem, result, quote (template in `12-case-study-template.md`).

**[FAQ]**
- *Do you record for us?* No. We work from your existing recordings: Riverside, Zoom, SquadCast or a camera.
- *How do you choose which moments to clip?* We look for a strong hook, a contrarian or useful idea, and a complete thought that lasts 30–60 seconds, then we refine based on what performs.
- *What if I already have an editor?* We can do clips only, working alongside them.
- *Is there a contract?* A 3-month initial term (podcasts take time to grow), then month to month.

**[Final CTA]**
## Want to see it first? We'll clip your latest episode for free.
**[Get my 2 free clips →]**: form fields are name, email, show link and "what do you want the podcast to do for your business?"

---

## Technical checklist
- [ ] Form submissions go into the CRM: export them weekly as CSV and import, or connect via Zapier.
- [ ] Booking link (Cal.com or Calendly) with 2–3 qualifying questions
- [ ] Analytics (Plausible, GA4 or Vercel Analytics), with a conversion event on form submit and booking
- [ ] The page loads in under 2 seconds on mobile, and videos are lazy-loaded
- [ ] A privacy policy page, plus a cookie notice if you target the EU or UK
- [ ] One page, one goal, no navigation menu
