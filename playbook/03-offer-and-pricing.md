# 3. Offer and pricing

## The core promise
> **"Launch-ready product videos and founder content every month, from one team that already knows your product. Short-form in 72 hours, feature videos in 10 working days."**

## Entry offer: Feature Launch Video (the pilot)
Most startups won't sign a monthly plan before seeing your work on *their* product. The pilot de-risks the first deal.

| | |
|---|---|
| **What** | One 45–60 second motion video of a feature or product (UI animation, captions, music, sound design), plus a 9:16 and a 1:1 cut-down for social |
| **Price** | **₹45,000 + GST**, fixed. 50% on order, 50% on delivery. |
| **Timeline** | 10 working days from receiving access to the product or screenshots |
| **Revisions** | 2 rounds |
| **Credit** | **The full ₹45,000 is credited** against the first month of any plan started within 30 days of delivery |

Why ₹45,000: it sits just under the ₹50,000–₹1,00,000 that Indian studios charge for a 60-second SaaS video, and the credit makes upgrading to a plan feel free.

### Add-ons
- Extra short-form video: ₹4,000
- Extra motion piece (≤60s): ₹35,000
- Rush delivery (24h short-form / 5-day motion): +30%
- Script and storyboard-only engagement: ₹15,000
- Shoot day (founder content, Bengaluru/your city): quote separately, with crew and travel at cost

### Discounts (the only ones you ever give)
- **Founding clients (first 5 only):** 15% off for the first 3 months, in exchange for permission to publish a case study and a short video testimonial.
- **Quarterly prepay:** 5% off.
- Never discount the pilot, and never discount to win a negotiation. Remove scope instead (for example, Growth → Starter).

## Scope rules (put these in every proposal and contract)
- A "short-form video" is up to 90 seconds, edited from footage or screen recordings the client supplies.
- A "motion piece" is up to 60 seconds, animated from a script approved before production starts.
- Unused deliverables roll over for **one** month only.
- A revision is a change to an approved script or storyboard direction. A new direction is a new request.
- The client provides footage, product access or screenshots, brand assets and one decision-maker for approvals within 3 working days.
- Turnaround starts when the brief and assets are complete.

## Payment terms
- Invoices go out on the 1st, payable in advance, due within 7 days. Pilot is 50/50.
- All prices exclude 18% GST. Register for GST before your first invoice if you aren't already registered. Check the SAC code and the place-of-supply rules with your accountant.
- Pause anytime after the minimum term with 15 days' notice before the next billing date.
- Late payment: work pauses after 7 days overdue. Say this politely in the contract.

## Unit economics (fill in with your real costs)
Aim for a **gross margin of 50% or more** on every plan. Track it per client every month:

| Plan | Price | Your delivery cost (editors, motion designers, tools, music licences) | Gross margin target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | ₹55,000 | ≤ ₹27,500 | ≥ 50% |
| Growth | ₹95,000 | ≤ ₹47,500 | ≥ 50% |
| Scale | ₹1,60,000 | ≤ ₹80,000 | ≥ 50% |

If one client's delivery cost goes above the target two months in a row, they're asking for more than the plan includes. Reset scope on the next monthly call, or move them up a plan.

Only offer this on the pilot, and only after the script has been approved in writing.
