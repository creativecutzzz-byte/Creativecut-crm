# 12. Case study template

Rules: **real numbers only, with the client's written approval** before anything is published. The CRM asks for permission at day 60.

## Data to collect (send this list with the `case_study_ask` email)
- Followers, views and posting frequency **before** you started (screenshot at kickoff; make this a habit from day 1)
- The same numbers **after** 60–90 days
- Best-performing clip: views, comments, shares
- Business outcomes: inbound DMs, sales calls or deals they can trace to clips
- Hours a week the client saved
- A direct quote

## Template (about 250 words, plus a one-card version for the landing page)

### How [Client] turned a quiet podcast into [result]
**Client:** [Name], [role] at [Company], a [one-line description]
**Show:** [Show name], [frequency]

**The problem.** [Client] had recorded [X] episodes but [posted almost no short-form / spent 8 hours a week editing / was relying on AI auto-clips that didn't reflect the brand]. *"[Quote about the pain]"*

**What we did.**
- [Plan], [X] episodes a month into [Y] clips and [Z] long-form edits
- Built a style guide around [their hook style / brand look]
- Tested [format] and [platform]; doubled down on [what worked]

**The results (in [N] days).**
- **[Metric 1]**, e.g. LinkedIn followers from 1,200 to 4,800
- **[Metric 2]**, e.g. best clip 85k views
- **[Business metric]**, e.g. 6 inbound sales calls traced to clips
- **[Time]**, e.g. 8 hours a week back

*"[Quote about the result]"*, [Name], [Title]

**[CTA]** Want the same for your show? Get 2 free clips from your latest episode →

### Card version (landing page or ad)
> **[Company]:** [Metric 1] in [N] days. "[One-line quote]," [Name], [Title]

## Before your first client: what to use for proof
- **Spec work:** re-edit 3 public episodes from your niche and show before/after. Label it clearly as "spec work, not a client".
- **The founding-client deal** (see `03-offer-and-pricing.md`) gets you your first real case studies by day 60.
