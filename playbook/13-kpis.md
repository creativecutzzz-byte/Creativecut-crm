# 13. Tracking performance and improving conversion

## Weekly scorecard (every Friday, 20 minutes; most of it is on the CRM dashboard)
| Metric | Where | Target | If it's under target… |
|---|---|---|---|
| New prospects added | Leads | 100/week | Block 90 minutes a day for prospecting; batch it |
| Reply rate (contacted → replied) | Dashboard funnel | 5–10% (15%+ with samples) | Deliverability first, then targeting, then personalization, then copy (`05-cold-email-sequences.md`) |
| Replied → call booked | Funnel | 40%+ | Reply faster (within 1 hour); offer 2 specific times plus the link |
| Call → proposal | Funnel | 70%+ | Qualify harder before booking; follow the script |
| Proposal → won (close rate) | Funnel | 30%+ | Send the proposal within 2 hours; use one plan; include samples from their niche; do the day-5 extra-clip follow-up |
| Follow-ups overdue | Dashboard | 0 | Clear them every morning before anything else |
| MRR | Dashboard | +$2k/month in months 1–6 | — |
| Client churn (monthly) | Dashboard | < 5%/month | Health checks, monthly reports (`11-onboarding-and-retention.md`) |
| On-time delivery (48h) | Your editor board | 95%+ | More editor capacity; tighter QA checklist |
| Gross margin per client | Hours × rate vs price | 50%+ | Templates, AI first pass, reprice |

## Unit economics to know by heart
- **CAC** (cost to acquire a customer) = (tools + samples + ads + your outbound hours × $/hour) ÷ new clients.
- **LTV** (lifetime value) = average MRR per client × gross margin × average months retained. Example: $1,997 × 50% × 9 months ≈ **$9,000**.
- Keep **LTV:CAC at 3:1 or better**, and earn back the cost of each customer within **3 months** or less.

## Monthly review (first Monday)
1. Export the CRM CSV. Group leads by **Source** and by **Niche**: which give the best reply rate and close rate? Do more of what wins.
2. Read every **Lost** lead's notes. What are the top 3 loss reasons? Fix one (offer, copy or proof).
3. Look at each client's health and renewal date. Anyone "at risk" gets a call this week.
4. Update `02-competition.md` with anything new you heard on calls.

## Conversion-rate experiments (run one at a time, for at least 2 weeks or 200 sends)
| Stage | Experiment |
|---|---|
| Cold email | Send the sample clip in email 1 instead of offering it; try a 2-word subject line; change the personalization angle (a moment from the episode vs. a gap in their channel) |
| Landing page | "Get 2 free clips" vs "Book a call" as the main button; show pricing vs hide it; add a before/after video in the hero |
| Call | Show the sample clip in the first 2 minutes vs at the end |
| Proposal | Founding-client price vs a free first week; Loom walkthrough vs PDF only |
| Retention | Monthly report as a Loom video vs a PDF |
