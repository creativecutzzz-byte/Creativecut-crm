# 12. Case studies

Case studies are your strongest sales asset in this niche. You can start today: you already have **43 projects**, including **17 motion and SaaS explainer projects**.

## Step 1: Mine the existing portfolio (this week)
1. List the 17 motion and SaaS projects. For each one, note: client, industry, what you made, and whether you have any result (views, launch outcome, a client quote).
2. Pick the **3 strongest** SaaS or tech pieces.
3. Ask each client for permission. Use this message:
> "Hi [name], we're putting together a few case studies on our SaaS work and the [project] we did for you is one of our favourites. Would you be OK with us featuring it, with your company name and a one-line quote? Happy to send the draft for approval first. If you'd prefer, we can feature it anonymously ('a Series A fintech')."
4. If they say no, or you can't reach them: publish it **anonymously**, with no logo and a general description. **Never** publish a client's name, logo or numbers without written permission.

## Step 2: Write each case study (template)
**Title:** How [Company / "a Series A AI startup"] [outcome] with [what you made]

| Section | What to write | Length |
|---|---|---|
| **Snapshot** | Client type · what we made · timeline · one headline result | 4 lines |
| **The challenge** | What they were launching, and why a screenshot or nothing wasn't working. Use their words. | 2–3 sentences |
| **What we did** | Script → style frames → animation → cut-downs. Mention any constraint you solved (tight deadline, complex product). | 3–5 bullets |
| **The result** | Only real numbers they've confirmed (views, demo requests, launch engagement, time saved), or a quote if there are no numbers | 2–4 bullets |
| **Quote** | 1–2 sentences from the client, approved | 1–2 lines |
| **The work** | Embed the video, plus 2–3 stills | — |
| **Call to action** | "Launching something? Get a free 15-second teaser." → landing page | 1 line |

### Questions to ask the client for results
- "What did you use the video for: launch post, website, sales calls, investor deck?"
- "Did you notice any change: engagement on the launch post, demo requests, fewer 'what does it do?' questions on calls?"
- "Roughly how much time did it save you compared with your previous approach?"
- "What would you tell another founder considering us?"

## Step 3: Use each case study in 5 places
1. A post on your LinkedIn (content pillar 4)
2. A section on the landing page (section 08)
3. Email 3 of the cold sequence (swap it in for the portfolio link)
4. Slide 2 of your sales-call screen share
5. The proposal's "Why Creative Cut" section
