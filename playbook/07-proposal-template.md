# 7. Sales proposal template

Send it within **2 hours of the call**, as a 2-page PDF or a shared doc, together with the "After the call" email from the CRM. Keep it short; the call did the selling.

---

## [Client company] × Creativecut
**Turning [Show name] into a weekly growth engine**
Prepared for [Name], [Date]. Valid for 14 days.

### 1. Where you are now
*(Use their words from the call.)*
- You publish [weekly] episodes of [Show name] and they take about [X] hours a week of your time to get out.
- Short-form clips: [none / inconsistent / AI auto-clips].
- What it's costing you: [missed posts, X hours a week, the show isn't bringing in leads].

### 2. Where you want to be in 6 months
- [Goal 1, e.g. 2–3 inbound sales conversations a month that come from the podcast]
- [Goal 2, e.g. post consistently on LinkedIn and Shorts without spending your own time on it]
- [How you'll measure it]

### 3. What we'll do
Each week, you upload your raw recording to the shared folder. Within 48 hours you get:
- 1 fully edited YouTube episode with a custom thumbnail, title and description
- 6 vertical clips with captions, a hook in the first 2 seconds, and a platform-ready caption for each
- Every month: a one-page "what worked" report and next month's plan

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Day 0 | Agreement and first payment |

| Day 30 | First monthly report |
| Day 75 | Review results and plan the next term |

3-month initial term, then month to month with 30 days' notice.

- **2 revision rounds** on every video.
- **You own everything we make for you.**

### 7. Next step
Reply **"approved"** and we'll send the agreement and invoice the same day. We'll hold a kickoff slot on [date].

[Your name], Founder, Creativecut · [email] · [booking link]

- [ ] Their words in sections 1 and 2, not generic ones
