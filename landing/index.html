FAQ

Questions founders ask.

Can't AI tools do this now?

For quick walkthroughs, often yes, and we use AI where it genuinely helps. What they can't do is decide the story: what to show, in what order, for which buyer. That's what makes a launch video work.

How fast is delivery?

Short-form in 48–72 hours depending on your plan. Motion pieces in about 10 working days, from an approved script. Rush delivery is available.

What do you need from us?

Product access or screenshots, brand assets, footage for founder content (we'll send a recording guide), and one person who approves within 3 working days.

Do you write the scripts?

Yes. Every motion piece starts with a script and style frames you approve before animation begins.

Is there a lock-in?

Plans have a 3-month minimum, because the first month is mostly learning your product. After that it's month-to-month with 15 days' notice. The pilot has no commitment.

Do you invoice with GST?

Yes. All prices are in INR plus 18% GST, and invoiced monthly in advance.
