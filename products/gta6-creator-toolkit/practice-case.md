## Stage 7 — Reward calculation (solved, fictional numbers)

Crop B on YouTube Shorts reaches **48,600 verified views** during its earning window.

```
Views ÷ 1,000      = 48,600 ÷ 1,000 = 48.6
× rate $1.00       = $48.60 gross
Minimum $3.00?     = yes, above it
Maximum $150.00?   = no, below it, so the clip keeps $48.60
Fee 10%            = − $4.86
Net to balance     = $43.74
```

Then: Content Rewards-style balance → Whop balance → bank/wallet. Methods and any withdrawal fee depend on the country and are **not verified for India** (see `sources.md` 3.4).
