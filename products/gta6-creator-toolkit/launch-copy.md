## 1. Product listing — Creator Resources

**Page title:** GTA 6 Creator Toolkit

**Pain hook (headline):** You have a 3-hour stream. Where is the 30-second Short?

**Support line:** Using just your phone, pick the right timestamp, make a vertical edit, and check it against the campaign brief before you submit.

**CTA button:** Get the GTA 6 Creator Toolkit · ₹199

**Short description (≤160 chars, for cards/SEO):**
A 36-page phone-first guide to making your first GTA 6 Short with free tools, and checking it against a clipping campaign brief before you submit.

**Body copy:**

Most first clips get rejected for something simple. A cropped-out overlay. The wrong platform. A link submitted too late.

The GTA 6 Creator Toolkit walks you through one practice clip from start to finish, on your phone:

- **Choose a campaign:** what to check in a brief before you edit, including payout setup from India.
- **Find three moments:** read a timestamped transcript and pick the hook, context and payoff.
- **Plan one Short:** three ready treatments and a 15–35 second plan.
- **Edit on a phone:** trim, vertical 9:16, captions in the safe zone, export, using free apps.
- **Check the brief:** a manual pre-submission checklist so the clip isn't rejected.
- **Publish and submit:** post copy, link formats, what each status means, and how to fix rejections.
- **Understand earnings:** how CPM is actually calculated (gross, fees, minimums, caps), what YouTube monetization really requires, and how to pitch a streamer for a paid trial.

**You don't need the game or a console.** Every exercise works from the PDF using a fictional practice stream, an original transcript and drawn frames.

**Also inside:** 2 copy-paste AI prompts (find highlights, check your plan against a brief), a submission and reward tracker, a troubleshooting decision page, a streamer outreach DM, a 7-day practice sprint, and an optional console streaming setup.

**What it is not:** a promise of approval, views or income. The toolkit teaches a skill and a checklist. Campaign rules, rates and payouts are set by each campaign and change often. The book shows you how to check them.

**Format:** PDF, 36 A5 pages, made for phone screens. Instant download after payment.
**Published by:** Creative Cut. An independent guide, not affiliated with Rockstar Games, Take-Two, Content Rewards, Whop or YouTube.

**FAQ**
- *Do I need GTA 6 or a PS5?* No. You practise with the book's fictional stream. For real clips you need footage you're allowed to use, from a campaign's source, your own stream or a client.
- *Which apps?* Free phone editors such as Edits and YouTube Create. The book shows a test to check any app for watermarks and export size.
- *Will I earn money?* That depends on campaign approval, views, rules and payout setup. The book explains each one; it can't promise results.
- *Is it in Hindi?* This edition is in simple English.
- *Refunds?* [Owner to set the policy before launch.]
