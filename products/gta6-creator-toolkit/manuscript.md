# Quick start: your first 10 minutes

You need a phone, a free editing app, and footage you are **allowed** to use. You do not need the game or a console.

<div class="steps" markdown="1">

1. **Read page 4** and guess which crop fails. Don't peek.
2. **Install one free editor:** Edits (Android and iPhone) or YouTube Create (Android in India). Page 14 explains both.
3. **Export a 10-second test** from the app. Check that it is vertical, has no watermark, and plays with sound.
4. **Open the practice transcript** on page 8. Circle one line that makes you smile.
5. **Make a note** of the time on that line. That is your first timestamp.

</div>

# Choose a campaign

A **clipping campaign** is a paid offer. A streamer, brand or agency puts money in a budget. Clippers cut Shorts from approved footage and post them on their own accounts. They get paid by views if the clip follows the **brief**, the written rules of the campaign.

**Read the brief before you edit.** Most rejections come from the brief, not from bad editing.

## The 8 things to check

1. **Source:** which footage you may use.
2. **Required elements:** overlay, tags, links, disclosure.
3. **Platforms** that pay (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok…).
4. **Rate**, plus the **minimum** and **maximum** payout.
5. **Budget left**, because when it runs out, earning stops.
6. **Deadline**, or "none shown".
7. **Eligibility:** age, account type, country.
8. **How you get paid** from your country.

[View the brief](https://contentrewards.com/c/campaigns/2eaa14fb-6927-4136-897e-b096e0d907d6).

# Find three moments

A **timestamped transcript** is the stream's words written out with the time each line was said. It lets you search 3 hours of video in 3 minutes.

Times are written **HH:MM:SS** (hours:minutes:seconds). So 01:12:08 means 1 hour, 12 minutes, 8 seconds into the stream.

{{fig doodle-timestamp-loop.svg | A pencil loop around 01:12:08, where the hook begins. Fictional transcript.}}

## What you are looking for

- **Hook:** the first line that makes someone stop scrolling. *"I am a professional driver."*
- **Context:** the least a new viewer needs to get it. *He is trying to park.*
- **Payoff:** the laugh, the shock or the "wow". *The crash and "Not the mangoes!"*

Good moments have all three within about 30 seconds. Look for sudden changes: a shout, a long silence, a laugh, or chat going wild.

## Transcripts miss things

A transcript only has words. It cannot show a face, a crash or a view. Moment B is the warning sign:

```text
01:47:49  REXA: [inaudible] — wait — [inaudible]
01:47:55  [long silence, 6 seconds]
```

The payoff happens in that silence. You **must watch it**.

## The 60-second check (for every moment)

1. Jump to 10 seconds **before** the start time.
2. **Watch with sound** to 10 seconds after the end.
3. Ask: is the payoff on screen? Is the audio clear?
4. Check for story spoilers and anything the brief bans.
5. Mark it **Keep**, **Maybe** or **Drop**.

# Plan one Short

The same stream can give you three kinds of Short. Pick the one your moment fits.

<div class="cards">
<div class="card c1"><h4>Funny reaction</h4><p><b>Hook:</b> a bold claim. "I'm a professional driver."</p><p><b>Context:</b> what he's trying to do.</p><p><b>Payoff:</b> it goes wrong, and he reacts.</p></div>
<div class="card c2"><h4>Unexpected moment</h4><p><b>Hook:</b> a question. "Is he following me?"</p><p><b>Context:</b> 1–2 lines of build-up.</p><p><b>Payoff:</b> the surprise, then the reaction.</p></div>
<div class="card c3"><h4>Spoiler-free discovery</h4><p><b>Hook:</b> "Wait. Look at this."</p><p><b>Context:</b> where he is, in a few words, no story.</p><p><b>Payoff:</b> the view and his honest reaction.</p></div>
</div>

## Three rules for any plan

1. **The hook is in the first 2 seconds.** Cut everything before it.
2. **Context is as short as possible.** One line, or a short text on screen.
3. **End on the payoff.** Stop right after the laugh or the "wow". Don't fade out slowly.

# Edit on a phone

## Pick one free app

- **Edits** (Instagram), Android and iPhone, free. Its store page says **4K export, no watermark**. It may ask for an Instagram login.
- **YouTube Create**, free, listed for India. Needs Android with **4 GB+ RAM**, or iPhone XR+ on iOS 17+.
- **Fallback:** any editor that passes the **10-second test** (1080×1920, no watermark, plays with sound). Never sideload an app that isn't in your store.

{{fig phone-trim.svg | A generic editor; button names differ by app. Illustration.}}

## Open and trim

1. Tap **New project** and choose your allowed source file.
2. Play to your **cut-in** time. Tap **Split** (or drag the start handle).
3. Play to your **cut-out** time. Split again. Delete the rest.
4. Check the length matches your plan (23 s).

<!-- PAGE 15 id=m4-reframe class=page title="Mission 4: Reframe" -->

<p class="kicker">Mission 4 · Reframe</p>

## Make it vertical (9:16)

**Aspect ratio** is the shape of the video. Streams are **16:9** (wide). Shorts and Reels are **9:16** (tall, 1080 px wide × 1920 px high).

{{fig phone-reframe.svg | The stacked layout: facecam on top, full gameplay in the middle, overlay kept. Illustration.}}

## Build the stacked layout

1. Set the project to **9:16**.
2. Add the clip **twice**. The second copy goes on an overlay track (sometimes called *picture-in-picture* or *video overlay*).
3. **Top copy:** pinch to zoom into the facecam. Fill the top third.
4. **Middle copy:** fit the **whole** 16:9 frame to the width. Do not zoom in. This keeps every corner, so the overlay stays.
5. Leave the bottom area for captions.

<div class="warn" markdown="1">

**Zooming in is how overlays disappear.** If the brief requires an overlay, check every corner after any crop.

</div>

<!-- PAGE 16 id=m4-finish class=page title="Mission 4: Captions and export" -->

<p class="kicker">Mission 4 · Captions, audio, export</p>

## Captions people can read

{{fig phone-captions.svg | Green = safe for captions. Pink = covered by app buttons. Illustration.}}

1. Tap **Text → Auto captions**. Then **read every word** and fix mistakes.
2. **3–5 words per line**, big, bold, dark outline.
3. Place captions in the **safe zone**: above the bottom fifth and away from the right edge.
4. Highlight **one keyword** per line (MANGOES).

## Audio, export, playback

- **Audio:** voice clear, game lower, crash louder. Mute music the brief doesn't allow.
- **Export:** 1080×1920, 30 fps or more. Highest quality.
- **Playback:** watch the export **start to end, with sound**: hook in 2 s, no typos, overlay visible, no watermark.

<div class="output" markdown="1">

**You now have:** an exported vertical Short that matches your plan.

</div>

# Check the brief: the reveal

Back on page 4, you picked A or B. **Crop A fails.**

{{fig crop-a-annotated.svg | Crop A, annotated. The centre crop cut off the required LIVE · /rexalive badge. Illustration.}}

Crop A looks great, but it is a **centre crop**: it keeps only the middle slice of the 16:9 frame. The required badge sits in the **top-right corner**, outside the slice. The brief said "visible for the **whole** clip", so a reviewer can rightly reject it.

<!-- PAGE 18 id=m5-fix class=page title="Mission 5: The fix" -->

<p class="kicker">Mission 5 · The fix</p>

## Crop B passes

{{fig crop-b-annotated.svg | Crop B, annotated. The stacked layout keeps the badge in its corner, unchanged. Illustration.}}

**The fix in one sentence:** switch to the stacked layout, so the full 16:9 frame (overlay included) sits in the middle and the facecam goes on top.

## Why not just slide the crop right?

Sliding the crop to the right might show the badge. But then you lose the left side, where the facecam is, and part of the action. The badge must also stay unchanged. Don't resize it, cover it or blur it. The stacked layout solves all of this at once.

<p class="kicker">Mission 6 · After you submit</p>

## The three statuses

<div class="statusflow">
<div class="st pending"><b>Pending</b><span>Waiting for the campaign team. On CPM, views count, but no money is set aside yet.</span></div>
<div class="st approved"><b>Approved</b><span>Money is set aside from the budget. Pays in rounds after a short hold.</span></div>
<div class="st rejected"><b>Rejected</b><span>A reason is shown. Nothing is paid. You can appeal once.</span></div>
</div>

On Content Rewards, **people review every clip**. Nothing is approved automatically, and the campaign's team sets the pace. On CPM, clips sent since 25 Sep 2026 pay in rounds: 7 days of earning plus a 3-day hold, about every 10 days, until 30 days after you sent it.

## What to do

- **Pending:** keep posting new clips. Don't resubmit the same post. Re-read the brief for a review condition (minimum views, time live, analytics).
- **Approved:** log it in your tracker (page 28). Keep the post up and unchanged.
- **Rejected:** read the reason. Fix the *next* clip. Appeal only if the reason is not in the written brief.

<!-- PAGE 22 id=m6-trouble class=page title="Mission 6: Troubleshooting" -->

<p class="kicker">Mission 6 · Troubleshooting</p>

## Four common problems

<span class="tag-fiction">Fictional examples</span>

**1. "Missing required overlay."** Rexa rejects a center-cropped clip. *Fix:* you can't edit a submitted post into a new one. Re-cut with the stacked layout, post it fresh and submit the new link.

**2. "Ineligible source footage."** A clipper used a different streamer's VOD. *Fix:* only use sources named in the brief. When in doubt, ask Support on the campaign page **before** editing.

**3. Missed deadline.** The post went up at 12:20 am, after the 11:59 pm deadline. *Fix:* set a phone alarm 2 hours before. Deadlines may be in another time zone, so convert to IST.

**4. Stalled approval.** Pending for 6 days. *Fix:* check the brief for a review condition. Then message the team through **Support** on the campaign page with your link and submit date. If the budget runs out before approval, there may be nothing to pay you from until the brand adds more.

**Low views?** Fix the first 2 seconds. Test a new hook on the next clip, not the same one. Post at the time your audience is awake.

# Three earning routes

## Route A: campaign rewards

**CPM** (*cost per mille*) is the rate per **1,000 eligible or verified views**, as the brief defines them. **Budget** is the campaign's total money for *everyone*, not what you will earn.

{{fig doodle-receipt.svg | Fictional receipt for the practice clip.}}

**The basic sum:** at $1 per 1,000 views, **100,000 eligible views = $100 gross**, before fees, caps and approval.

## When the number is lower

- **Below minimum:** 2,100 views = $2.10 < $3, so $0 unless more views arrive in time.
- **Cap:** 210,000 views = $210, capped at $150. After the 10% fee, $135.
- **Budget runs out:** new views stop earning until a top-up.
- **Pending:** "Queued" or "Arriving" money can't be withdrawn yet.

**Your turn:** 7,400 views at $1/1K? *(Answer: $7.40 − $0.74 fee = $6.66.)*

<!-- PAGE 24 id=m7-youtube class=page title="Mission 7: YouTube monetization" -->

<p class="kicker">Mission 7 · Route B</p>

## YouTube monetization

The **YouTube Partner Program (YPP)** shares ad money with creators. To apply for ad revenue you need **1,000 subscribers** plus either **4,000 public watch hours** in 12 months or **10 million Shorts views** in 90 days. Shorts-feed views do **not** count toward the 4,000 hours. (Checked 19 Nov 2026.)

There is also a lower tier: 500 subscribers, 3 uploads in 90 days, and 3,000 hours or 3 million Shorts views. India is eligible. It unlocks fan funding and Shopping, **not** ad revenue.

Meeting the numbers isn't enough. You apply in **YouTube Studio → Earn**, then YouTube reviews the whole channel. This usually takes about a month.

<div class="warn" markdown="1">

**Permission ≠ monetization.** YouTube's *reused content* policy applies **even if you have permission from the original creator**. A channel of pure campaign clips can be refused ads. Campaign approval doesn't make a channel eligible.

</div>

**You now have:** your next route. Pick **A** (join one campaign), **B** (plan one narrated breakdown) or **C** (send one outreach DM, page 30).

</div>

## Find timestamped highlights

Paste this into any chat assistant, then paste your transcript under it. Works as plain text. No setup needed.

```prompt
You are helping me find Short-form highlights in a livestream transcript.
Rules:
- Use ONLY timestamps that appear in the transcript. Never invent or round a time.
- Quote the exact dialogue word for word.
- Return the 3 best moments, each 15-35 seconds long.
For each moment give:
1. Start time and end time (copied from the transcript)
2. Exact quoted dialogue
3. Hook (the first line that makes people stop scrolling)
4. Context (the least a new viewer needs)
5. Payoff (the laugh, surprise or wow)
6. Type: funny reaction / unexpected moment / spoiler-free discovery
7. "NEEDS VISUAL CHECK" if the payoff is silent, inaudible,
   or a sound effect, or if it might be a story spoiler.
If fewer than 3 good moments exist, say so. Do not pad.
TRANSCRIPT:
[paste here]
```

<div class="tip" markdown="1">

**Check the result.** AI can get times wrong. Search the transcript for every time it gives you, then watch each moment (the 60-second check on page 9).

</div>

## Submission and reward tracker

Make one row per submitted post in Google Sheets or your notes app. Use these column headings:

```text
DATE | CAMPAIGN | LINK | STATUS | VERIFIED VIEWS | EST. GROSS | DEDUCTIONS | ACTUAL PAYOUT
```

**Worked row** <span class="tag-fiction">Fictional</span>

| Column | Example |
|---|---|
| Date | 21 Nov 2026 |
| Campaign | Rexa Live (fictional) |
| Link | youtube.com/shorts/XXXXXXXXXXX |
| Status | Approved |
| Verified views | 48,600 |
| Est. gross | $48.60 |
| Deductions | −$4.86 (10% fee) |
| Actual payout | $43.74, once Received |

**Formulas (Google Sheets).** Put views in E, rate per 1,000 in J, fee % in K, max in L, min in M:

```text
Est. gross:  =MIN(E2/1000*J2, L2)
Pays?:       =IF(E2/1000*J2>=M2,"yes","below min")
Deductions:  =F2*K2
Net:         =F2-G2
```

<div class="tip" markdown="1">

**Estimated is not actual.** Fill "Actual payout" only once the money shows as **Received**.

<!-- PAGE 29 id=decide class=resource title="Troubleshooting decisions" -->

<p class="kicker">Copy-and-use · Decisions</p>

## What do I do now?

<div class="decide" markdown="1">

### Rejected
1. Is the reason in the written brief? **Yes →** fix the next clip, don't appeal. **No →** appeal once, politely, quoting the brief.
2. Was it a missing overlay or tag? → Re-cut, post fresh, submit the new link.
3. Wrong source footage? → Stop. Use only listed sources.

### Low views
1. Did people leave in the first 2 s? → Make a new hook. Start on the funniest line.
2. Captions hard to read? → Bigger, fewer words, inside the safe zone.
3. Same moment as 50 other clippers? → Pick a less obvious moment.
4. Under the minimum payout? → It may pay $0. Move on to the next clip.

### Pending payout
1. Is it **Queued** (still earning or waiting for review)? → Wait. Check the brief's review conditions.
2. Is it **Arriving** (in the hold)? → Wait for the date shown.
3. Is it **Received**? → Withdraw to Whop, then to your payout method.
4. Has nothing arrived a day after withdrawing? → Contact support with the amount and time.

</div>

## Console streaming setup

Only for readers who own a console and want to stream and clip **their own** gameplay.

### Level 1: PS5 on its own
1. Download the **YouTube** or **Twitch** app on the PS5 and link your account.
2. Press **Create** on the controller and choose **Broadcast**.
3. **Double-tap Create** to save the last 30 seconds (the default). Change the length under Settings → Captures and Broadcasts → Shortcuts for Create Button.

*Source: PlayStation, checked 19 Nov 2026.*

### Level 2: capture card + OBS
**OBS Studio** is free streaming and recording software for PC and Mac. A **capture card** brings the console's video into the computer.

1. Connect the PS5 **directly to a TV** first.
2. Turn **HDCP off**: Settings → System → HDMI. (HDCP is copy protection that blocks capture.)
3. Connect PS5 → capture card → TV, with the card's USB going to the PC.
4. In OBS add a **Video Capture Device** source and select the card.

*Source: Elgato's PS5 setup guide, checked 19 Nov 2026.*

**Xbox:** this book could not verify Xbox's current broadcast options. Check Xbox Support.

## Glossary

<dl class="gloss">
<dt>9:16</dt><dd>Tall video shape used by Shorts and Reels (1080×1920).</dd>
<dt>Aspect ratio</dt><dd>The shape of a video: width compared with height.</dd>
<dt>Brief</dt><dd>The written rules of a campaign.</dd>
<dt>Budget</dt><dd>All the money a campaign has for every clipper, not just you.</dd>
<dt>Captions</dt><dd>On-screen words that match what is said.</dd>
<dt>Clipping</dt><dd>Cutting short videos from longer footage.</dd>
<dt>CPM</dt><dd>Pay per 1,000 eligible or verified views, as the brief defines them.</dd>
<dt>Export</dt><dd>Saving your edit as a finished video file.</dd>
<dt>Gross / net</dt><dd>Before fees / after fees.</dd>
<dt>HDCP</dt><dd>Copy protection on HDMI that blocks capture cards.</dd>
<dt>Hook</dt><dd>The first 1–2 seconds that stop the scroll.</dd>
<dt>KYC</dt><dd>"Know your customer": identity checks before payouts.</dd>
<dt>Max / min payout</dt><dd>The most one clip can earn / the least it must earn before it pays.</dd>
<dt>Overlay</dt><dd>A graphic on top of the video, like a streamer's badge.</dd>
<dt>Payoff</dt><dd>The laugh, surprise or wow at the end.</dd>
<dt>Reused content</dt><dd>YouTube's term for others' content without significant original input.</dd>
<dt>Safe zone</dt><dd>The screen area not covered by app buttons.</dd>
<dt>Timeline</dt><dd>The strip in an editor where clips sit in time order.</dd>
<dt>Timestamp</dt><dd>A time in the stream, written HH:MM:SS.</dd>
<dt>Trim</dt><dd>Cutting off the start or end of a clip.</dd>
<dt>VOD</dt><dd>"Video on demand": a saved recording of a livestream.</dd>
<dt>YPP</dt><dd>YouTube Partner Program, YouTube's monetization program.</dd>
</dl>
